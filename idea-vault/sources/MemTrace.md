MemTrace is a June 2026 preprint that probes 835 knowledge points across age, question type and evidence condition, with current, historical and change questions. It shows failures on both sides of retrieval, and that high abstention can hide poor correction. The report cites it as prior art for causal evaluation and as the reason a benchmark of previous values is not open ground.
