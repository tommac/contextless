For conversations, the natural unit to test is a question-answer pair expanded to its local reply chain, compared with fixed turns and topic segments under the same token budget. Reply chains and question-answer pairing are plausible and widely used, but the notes found little controlled evidence that isolates them for retrieval. Threads that are inferred need confidence handling, because exact recovery of conversations remains hard.
