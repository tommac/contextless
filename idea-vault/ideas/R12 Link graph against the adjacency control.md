The hypothesis is that same-value and answers edges help questions spanning several atoms beyond what expanding to adjacent turns gives. The local structural rate is measured first, then multi-hop probes are written, because the present single-source probes cannot show a benefit, and each expansion is treated as a priced candidate. It is falsified if adjacency ties the graph, or if the local structural rate is so low that few questions can benefit, and then the graph planned for Phase 3 is dropped.
