For a small system the most defensible route to publication is an evaluation contribution, such as an audit, a causal protocol or a cost-aware replication that changes a conclusion, and not a claim of a new best score. The renamed NeurIPS Evaluations and Datasets track welcomes audits, reproductions and negative results and does not require beating prior work. The report therefore aims for an evaluation-led first paper, and ranks a generic new benchmark as the weakest option in a crowded field.
