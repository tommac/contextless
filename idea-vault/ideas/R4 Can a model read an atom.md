The hypothesis is that a verbatim atom with its current header answers as well as the whole source turn once retrieval is taken out of the question, which the project names as its main risk. The known gold evidence is placed directly in four renderings: bare atom, atom with header, atom with neighbours, and whole turn. It is falsified if header-only atoms fall more than 8 points below whole turns, after which neighbour expansion becomes a priced default.
