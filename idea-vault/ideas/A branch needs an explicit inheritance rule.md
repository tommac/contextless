A useful system for tangents needs both a detected segment and an explicit rule for what a branch inherits. In a five-day field deployment of a branching interface, participants were unsure what a branch inherited, and the study had no comparison condition and no measure of output quality. The notes also found no measured evidence on which merge strategy works best when a branch's result returns to its parent.
