The research note found no benchmark that targets questions about what was tried for a subject, or how a plan changed, over real work logs such as coding sessions. LongMemEval multi-session and BEAM's event-ordering and summarization categories are the nearest proxies, and LoCoMo is a weak testbed because most of its questions cite a single piece of evidence. The research note ranks its five open mechanisms by expected value for exactly these two kinds of question, so the missing benchmark limits how any of them can be tested.
