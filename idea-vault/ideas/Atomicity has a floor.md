A unit of memory must be small enough to match precisely but must carry enough context to be understood. Work on molecular facts argues that fully atomic facts are the wrong target and sets two criteria, decontextuality and minimality. In proposition indexing a share of outputs did not stand alone and the index grew more than sixfold, which bears on how small this project's atoms should be.
