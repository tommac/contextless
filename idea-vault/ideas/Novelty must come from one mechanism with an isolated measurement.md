The combination of verbatim turns, model-free construction and deterministic retrieval is not novel as of late 2026: EdgeMem, Zero-Mem, SmartSearch's substring path and DMF already pair verbatim conversation with little or no model use. Novelty therefore has to come from a specific mechanism with an isolated measurement. The least occupied ideas with a plausible effect on multi-evidence questions are explicit-aspect slot allocation, a deterministic per-key change ledger, cross-span cover density, lexically seeded graph propagation without embeddings and a foraging-style stopping rule. Several of the 2026 sources behind this judgement are single-group preprints without independent replication, and the absence claims come from targeted searches run on one day.
