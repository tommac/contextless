The report judges prefix-priced context selection likely new but uncertain. The closest prior art is ClawVM's assembly by utility per token, Budget-Aware Routing, TokenPilot and Pricing the Context Window. What would be new is a joint optimizer with the prefix-closed constraint, validated against real bills and answer quality. The last of those papers prices cached tokens with a shadow price and could not be read, so it must be read before anyone claims priority.
