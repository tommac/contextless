Reporting a position on a LoCoMo leaderboard as the headline result is rejected. An audit finds its labels and its judge flawed, and its conversations are short enough to fit in a context window, so scores above the audited ceiling are not evidence of better memory. The effective sample is also small, because 1,540 questions come from only 10 conversations.
