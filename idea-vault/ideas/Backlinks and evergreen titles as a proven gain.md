Backlinks and evergreen note titles are often presented as a proven gain for LLM memory, but the notes found no controlled measurement of either. The nearest test adds header-chain prefixes to the chunks of one private knowledge base, and a separate result finds that real heading paths beat context prefixes written by a model. The decision is to test real structural keys first and to leave model-written titles for last.
