A claim of the same quality at lower cost requires a margin fixed in advance, such as the lower confidence bound of the difference staying above minus 3 points. A difference that is not significant is not evidence that two arms are equivalent. The samples needed are large: a margin of minus 8 points needs about 145 items at 15 percent discordance, and a margin of minus 5 needs about 495 at 20 percent.
