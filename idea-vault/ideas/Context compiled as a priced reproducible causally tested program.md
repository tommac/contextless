The report's proposed thesis is that context should be compiled as a priced, reproducible, causally tested program rather than stored as a smarter memory. The field has spent two years building more elaborate memories, and its best evidence now says the elaboration is often the problem. The open question becomes which verbatim bytes are worth paying for on this call, and the report accepts that the honest result may be a simpler system.
