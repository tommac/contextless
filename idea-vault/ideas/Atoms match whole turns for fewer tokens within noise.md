At the 8k default, ranked atoms found a few more answers than six whole turns, 107 against 103 of the 120 distinct questions, in packets 6 to 44 percent smaller. Per conversation the differences are one to three probes of 30 and a paired sign test gives p about 0.5, so the reading the project adopts is no worse for less, not better. Atoms are kept on that basis.
