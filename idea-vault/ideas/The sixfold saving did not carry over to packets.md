The first experiment measured the tokens spent, in rank order, before the answer was in, and atoms needed about six times fewer. A packet fills a fixed budget whatever the question, and vaguer questions need more of it, so in built packets the saving shrank to 6 to 44 percent at the same recovery. At about a third of the ranked tokens atoms were three probes behind whole turns, so Phase 1 missed its own bar as written.
