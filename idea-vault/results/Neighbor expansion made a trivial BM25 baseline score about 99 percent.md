FluctlightDB withdrew an earlier 99.0 percent result because expanding each hit by three session neighbours on either side credited adjacent spans, and under that policy a trivial BM25 baseline also scored about 99 percent. Expansion that is not charged to the budget can make any retriever look perfect. This project met the same failure in small: its first recall probe counted any paragraph of the source turn as a hit.
