In A-MEM's ablation with GPT-4o-mini, adding links to a version with no links or evolution raised multi-hop F1 from 9.65 to 21.35, and the full system reached 27.02. Single-hop F1 tripled when links were added. The notes caution that a threefold single-hop gain looks more like a weak no-link condition than a pure effect of linking, so a strong hybrid-retrieval control would be more informative.
