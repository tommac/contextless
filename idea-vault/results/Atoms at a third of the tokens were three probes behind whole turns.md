At the 4k budget, about a third of the ranked tokens, atoms found 127 of 150 against 130 for six whole turns, and 45 of 60 against 48 on the fresh probes. Both gaps are within noise, but Phase 1's own bar, to at least match turn probes at a third of the tokens on fresh probes, was missed as written. Atoms were kept because at 8k they do no worse for less.
