MemTrace, a 2026 preprint with 200,453 scored answers, found that 73.3 percent of sampled failures were cases where the evidence was reachable but the question went unsolved, against 7.0 percent where it was unreachable. Supplying gold evidence raised the reached-but-unsolved share further. Failures therefore sit on both sides of retrieval, and most sit after it.
