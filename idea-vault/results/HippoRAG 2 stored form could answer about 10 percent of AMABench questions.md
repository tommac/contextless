In an oracle probe from a peer-reviewed comparison, the form in which HippoRAG 2 stores a corpus could answer only about 10 percent of sampled AMABench questions. Its build cost needed about 32 shared questions to break even against a grep agent, against about 2 for a lighter system. Structure built at ingest can therefore destroy evidence before any retrieval happens.
