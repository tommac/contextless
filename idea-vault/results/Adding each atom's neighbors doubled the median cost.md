Sending the paragraph before and after each ranked atom doubled the median tokens spent before the answer was in. Recovery was unchanged within 8k and 16k but fell by 1 to 3 of 30 within 2k and by 1 to 4 of 30 within 4k, because the same budget holds about half as many hits. It stayed 2.5 to 3.5 times cheaper than whole turns and is the price of readability, which only an end-to-end test can weigh.
