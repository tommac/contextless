In a peer-reviewed comparison of twelve baselines, the value of an indexed memory tracked the structural rate, the share of questions no single retrieved unit can answer. On LoCoMo, where that rate is 60.7 percent, graph methods averaged 13.0 points above a grep agent, and on HotpotQA, where it is 17.7 percent, they averaged 15.4 points below it. Whether links pay therefore depends on the questions asked, which is why the local structural rate should be measured first.
