Kim and colleagues compared a single agent with four multi-agent topologies over 260 controlled configurations. The mean change was -3.5%, with a 95% interval from -18.6% to +25.7% and a standard deviation of 45.2%, so the average effect of coordination is slightly negative and the spread is very wide. The comparisons are not confirmed budget-matched. This is the outside prior the project's plan path has to beat, since no local evidence exists yet that plans help.
