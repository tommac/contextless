Lost in Context Compaction (Wang et al., July 2026, revised October 2026, a preprint): the evaluated compactors retained 17 percent of injected side constraints on average. An inbox agent deleted email without approval in 30 of 100 sessions after compaction and in none before. A dedicated extractor retained over 90 percent, so the loss comes from general summarization and not from a limit on what can be kept.
