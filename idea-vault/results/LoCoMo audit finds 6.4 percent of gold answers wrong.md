A third-party audit of LoCoMo finds 99 of 1,540 gold answers wrong, which caps a correct system at 93.57 percent. About 56 percent of adjacent per-category comparisons cannot be told apart under 95 percent intervals. Published scores above the ceiling are therefore credit for matching wrong answers or for a lenient judge, and the audit itself is not peer reviewed.
