In a pre-registered 2026 study, reranking's gain over cosine order was 17.4 points on LoCoMo and 9.1 on LongMemEval when 3 of 30 candidates were kept, falling to 1.5 and 1.1 when 20 of 30 were kept. The reranker is a typed decision model called per query over a cosine shortlist, so it needs a model at query time and embeddings. Reranking also lowered correct abstention in the same study.
