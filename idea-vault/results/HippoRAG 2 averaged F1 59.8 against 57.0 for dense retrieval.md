HippoRAG 2's own table gives an average QA F1 of 59.8 against 57.0 for a dense baseline, 49.6 for GraphRAG, 48.8 for RAPTOR and 6.6 for LightRAG. The biggest gain was on one dataset, 2Wiki, and the others were small. The authors themselves state that earlier structure-augmented methods underperform the strongest embedding retrieval.
