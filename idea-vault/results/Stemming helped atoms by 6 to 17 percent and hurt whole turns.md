In the first experiment the porter stemmer lowered the median cost of reaching the answer with atoms by 6 to 17 percent and raised that of whole turns by 2 to 25 percent, because a long turn matches more stray word forms. The effects are small and mixed, so stemming was adopted for the atom index only. The first run on real transcripts had traced some of its misses to paraphrase without stemming.
