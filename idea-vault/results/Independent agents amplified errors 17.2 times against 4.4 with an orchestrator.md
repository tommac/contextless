Relative to a single agent at 1.0, independent agents whose outputs were simply aggregated amplified errors 17.2 times, decentralized teams 7.8, hybrid 5.1 and a centralized orchestrator 4.4. Errors compound when nothing checks the join. A central checker reduces the amplification without removing it, and it is the costliest non-hybrid option in tokens.
