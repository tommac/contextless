MemTree's own results include cases where raw text wins: on MSC at 15 rounds full history scored 95.6 against 84.8 for MemTree, and on QuALITY full text beat MemTree and RAPTOR. On MSC-E at 200 rounds MemTree scored 82.5, above full history at 78.0. A summary tree therefore helped only once the history was very long.
