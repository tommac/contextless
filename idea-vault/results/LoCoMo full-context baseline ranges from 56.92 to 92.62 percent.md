The same full-context baseline on LoCoMo scores 56.92 percent in a 2026 preprint, 72.90 in Mem0's vendor paper and 92.62 in a third-party audit, depending on reader, judge and prompt. The 36-point spread is larger than almost any claimed system gain. A LoCoMo result without a full-context row from the same harness therefore cannot be interpreted.
