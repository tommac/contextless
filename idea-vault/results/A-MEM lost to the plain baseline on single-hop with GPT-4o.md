With GPT-4o, A-MEM's own peer-reviewed table shows it losing to the plain baseline on single-hop questions, 48.43 against 61.56, and on adversarial ones, 36.35 against 52.61. It won multi-hop, temporal and open-domain questions, most clearly temporal at 39.41 against 9.09. This is the report's reason to reject model-written notes as storage while accepting that links can help on questions that span several units.
