Doc2Query--, peer reviewed at ECIR 2023, filters hallucinated generated queries before they are indexed: up to 16 percent more effectiveness, 23 percent lower mean query time and a 33 percent smaller index than Doc2Query. The model runs at index time only and the inverted index is unchanged at query time. For atom titles and questions it argues for checking model-written index entries before they are indexed.
