Questions that share a conversation are correlated, which shrinks the effective sample. With illustrative intraclass correlations of 0.05 and 0.10, LoCoMo's 1,540 items in 10 conversations behave like about 178 and 94 independent items. The correlations are illustrative only, since the notes found no published values for memory benchmarks, so the real figure has to be estimated from pilot data.
