A peer-reviewed study separates a citation that is correct from one that is faithful, and reports up to 57 percent of citations unfaithful for one model in adversarial tests. The cause is post-rationalization: the model answers from prior knowledge and then attaches a source that fits. A quote check confirms that the text exists, not that the answer came from it, which is why citations must be tested causally before they are used as labels.
