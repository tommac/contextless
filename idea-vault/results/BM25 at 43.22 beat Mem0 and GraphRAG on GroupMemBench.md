On GroupMemBench, a third-party preprint, plain BM25 averaged 43.22, above HippoRAG at 39.72, A-Mem at 35.10, MemGPT at 28.15, Mem0 at 25.73 and GraphRAG at 20.56, with only Hindsight higher at 46.01. The authors attribute the result to ingestion erasing thread structure, speaker context and wording. The report reads it as showing that BM25 is a hard floor every lane must beat.
