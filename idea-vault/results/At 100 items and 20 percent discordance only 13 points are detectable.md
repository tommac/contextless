The research notes computed the power of the exact McNemar test for small paired experiments. With 100 items and 20 percent discordant pairs, the smallest difference detectable with 80 percent power is about 13 points, and with 30 to 100 items only large effects are detectable at all. Fewer than 6 discordant pairs can never reach significance with the exact test. This is the author's own calculation, not taken from a source.
