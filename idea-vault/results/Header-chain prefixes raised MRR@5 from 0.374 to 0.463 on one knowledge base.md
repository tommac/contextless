On one private Markdown knowledge base of 196,411 chunks and 1,600 queries, prefixing each chunk with its document and header chain raised MRR@5 from 0.374 to 0.463. This is the nearest evidence to the claim that evergreen titles help, and it tests hierarchy prefixes, not the style of note titles. The authors warn that the absolute scores can be artifacts, because agreement between the model annotators collapsed when the prefixes were removed.
