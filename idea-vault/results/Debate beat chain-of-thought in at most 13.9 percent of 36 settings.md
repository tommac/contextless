Zhang and colleagues (2025) re-evaluated five multi-agent debate frameworks on nine benchmarks with four models. Against chain-of-thought over 36 settings the frameworks won in 0% to 13.9%. The original 2023 debate gains were real against a single direct answer and were not budget-matched, and judge-based frameworks flip many initially correct answers to wrong. Mixing different models in a debate is the one variant with consistent support, which is the only indirect evidence for a reviewer on a different provider.
