In the controlled scaling study, token overhead against a single agent was 58% for independent agents, 263% decentralized, 285% centralized and 515% hybrid, described as 1.6 to 6.2 times. Successes per 1,000 tokens fell from 67.7 for a single agent to 13.6 for the hybrid topology. Other studies in the survey report 2 to over 20 times on coding and math pipelines, and one production system about 15 times a chat.
