In the confirming answer test of 2026-11-25 (120 held-out questions, rule fixed before any answer), the packet the engine builds with the map of checked notes, plus one recall request, stated 0.882 of the needed passages. The same engine with the map off, with the same one request, stated 0.706. The difference is 0.175, with 66 questions won and 4 lost. On the 29 questions that also had a whole-conversation answer the map packet was level with the whole conversation at under a quarter of the tokens. The map packet is twice the size of the map-off packet, and no arm filled those tokens with search results. The notes came from separate writers, not the indexer.
