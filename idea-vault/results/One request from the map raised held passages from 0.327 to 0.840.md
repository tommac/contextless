Before any request, the live packets with and without the map held the same 0.327 of the 588 needed passages word for word. After the model's one recall request the packet with the map held 0.840 and the one without 0.614. A held passage was stated in 0.95 to 0.97 of verdicts in every limited arm. So the map's gain in answers comes from the model choosing paragraphs well from the note lines, not from answering out of the lines themselves. Replaying all 240 requests with no model showed a real second packet holds every passage the test's model had.
