ContextBranch cut context from 31.0 to 13.0 messages and raised the overall judged score from 8.46 to 8.67, a gain of 2.5 percent. Focus and context awareness rose, while accuracy, relevance and specificity showed no significant change. The scenarios were synthetic, GPT-4 both generated and judged, and there were no human users and no token counts, so the gain cannot be separated from simply removing distractors.
