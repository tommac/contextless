Eywa runs deterministic detectors for dates, entities, monetary values, versions, identifiers, URLs, IP addresses, percentages and quoted strings. On chat data such hard anchors were sparse: 59 of 2,541 persisted facts in its main LoCoMo store carried one, and 67.4% of candidate facts in a 143-sample audit had none. The research note takes this as a warning that typed anchors cover a small share of chat-style facts, and assumes coverage should be higher in technical logs, which is still to be tested. It bounds what a value lane or a value ledger can reach on conversational benchmarks.
