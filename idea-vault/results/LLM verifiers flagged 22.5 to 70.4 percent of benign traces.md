In the Watching the Watchers study (NeurIPS 2026), a fault-injection test in a simulated procurement and finance workflow, the LLM verifier's false-positive rate on matched benign traces was 22.5% to 70.4%. Deterministic event-local rules reached AUROC 0.95 to 0.98 on local violations and were at chance on cross-event relations. The verifier's true-positive rates were not on the page read. The survey holds that the project's rule that a reviewer must quote what it objects to is the kind of grounding that separates useful verification from this regime.
