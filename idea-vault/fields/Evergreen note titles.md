Evergreen-note practice, cited from Andy Matuschak's working notes, gives every note a title that states its claim, so the title works like an API for the note. The design borrows this for atoms: a one-line claim per paragraph that is searched and shown beside the atom id. It also suggests Obsidian itself as a debugger for the atom graph.
