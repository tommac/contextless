Zettelkasten is a note-taking practice of small atomic notes joined by explicit links, popular in tools such as Obsidian. Several LLM memory systems borrow it, with a model writing both the notes and the links. The notes found measured support for linked notes on synthesis questions, but no study that isolates backlinks or note titles, no long test of an LLM wiki in real use, and no measurement of how fast model-maintained links rot.
