McNemar's test, from statistics, compares two treatments on the same items by looking only at the items where the two disagree. For paired binary outcomes the mid-p version is recommended over the exact conditional test, which is conservative. The notes use it for every planned comparison of two context arms on the same questions, and their power table follows from it.
